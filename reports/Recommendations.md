## Business Findings and Recommendations

### 1. Standard Class Drives the Largest Financial Losses
**Finding:**  
Standard Class is the dominant shipping mode and generated the highest total loss ($2.33M), largely due to its high order volume.

**Recommendation:**  
Conduct a targeted operational review of Standard Class fulfilment and delivery processes. Because Standard Class represents the largest share of shipments, even small efficiency improvements could have a substantial impact on reducing overall losses.

---

### 2. Shipping Mode Influences Delivery Performance
**Finding:**  
A chi-squared test revealed a statistically significant relationship between shipping mode and late-delivery risk, indicating that delivery performance varies meaningfully across shipping methods.

**Recommendation:**  
Implement shipping-mode-specific performance monitoring. Investigate carrier performance, warehouse operations, and route planning within shipping modes that exhibit elevated delay rates.

---

### 3. Late Deliveries Do Not Significantly Reduce Profitability
**Finding:**  
Independent t-test and Mann-Whitney U test results found no statistically significant difference in profit between late and on-time deliveries.

**Recommendation:**  
Do not evaluate delivery performance solely through profitability metrics. Introduce customer-centric KPIs such as customer satisfaction, repeat purchase rates, and retention metrics to assess the broader business impact of delivery delays.

---

### 4. First Class Generates the Highest Average Profit
**Finding:**  
First Class shipping produces the highest average profit per order among all shipping modes.

**Recommendation:**  
Explore opportunities to increase First Class adoption through promotional campaigns, pricing optimisation, or checkout upselling strategies.

---

### 5. Same Day Shipping Produces the Lowest Average Profit
**Finding:**  
Same Day shipping generates the lowest average profit per order.

**Recommendation:**  
Review the pricing structure and operational costs associated with Same Day deliveries to determine whether the service is appropriately priced relative to fulfilment expenses.

---

### 6. Shipping Cancellations Create the Largest Per-Order Losses
**Finding:**  
Cancelled shipments generate the greatest average loss per order.

**Recommendation:**  
Prioritise cancellation reduction initiatives by investigating common causes such as inventory shortages, payment issues, fulfilment delays, and customer order changes.

---

### 7. Profitability Varies Significantly by Month
**Finding:**  
Monthly profitability differs significantly, with October consistently producing the highest average profit.

**Recommendation:**  
Investigate the drivers behind October's performance, including seasonal demand patterns, promotional campaigns, and product mix. Apply successful strategies to lower-performing months where appropriate.

---

### 8. Significant Profit Outliers Exist
**Finding:**  
Outlier analysis identified 18,942 extreme profit and loss observations using the IQR method.

**Recommendation:**  
Perform a detailed investigation of extreme transactions to identify pricing anomalies, data quality issues, operational inefficiencies, or exceptional customer orders that may warrant further business action.

---

## Executive Summary

### Key Takeaways
- Standard Class shipments represent the largest opportunity for loss reduction.
- Shipping mode significantly affects delivery performance.
- Delivery delays do not appear to reduce per-order profitability directly.
- First Class shipping generates the highest average profit per order.
- Shipment cancellations are the largest driver of individual-order losses.
- October consistently demonstrates superior profitability performance.
